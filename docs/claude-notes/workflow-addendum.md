## Driving feel (`js/enh/feel.js`, `window.FEEL`; game.js step(); d3056a0)
Everything here follows ⚙️ Realistic driving (`REAL`); off = the old behaviour exactly (plow through buildings, pass through trees/traffic, 76 mph off-road cap). Tyre/wind noise plays with ⚙️ Sound in either case.
- **Surfaces.** `FEEL.surf` = the surface under the car, `FEEL.mu` its grip (blended, 4/s). On a road: `S.rcls` 'paved' or 'gravel' (set in the road detection: gravel if every road feature under the car is `surface=unpaved` or class track/path). Off road: `S.land` (raw land class set by `landHere`, or 'park') → `LANDS` table → grass (default) / lot (commercial, retail, industrial, school, hospital, campus, stadium — parking lots are polygons, not roads) / dirt (farmland, farmyard) / woods / sand / mud (wetland) / rock / ice / gravel (rail yard). Tractor mode = dirt (bumps and sound only).
  - Table `SURF`: mu, drag (per m/s), rr, cap (m/s, 0 = car's own), rough, dust colour, hum/grit levels, hum tone. Pavement 1/0/0/0; lot .95, cap 34; gravel .75, drag .02; grass .6, .12, cap 34; dirt .55, .18, cap 30; woods .55, .25, cap 22; sand .5, .28, cap 20; mud .4, .4, cap 14; rock .65, .1, cap 24; ice .3.
  - game.js: `grip()` = `wxGrip()` (weather, the old grip()) × `FEEL.mu`; so braking, launch, `steerLock` and the autopilot all feel the surface. The wet/slick toast uses `wxGrip()` only. Drag: `a -= v·drag + sign·rr`; cap from the surface.
  - Simulated (Explorer, speeds mph, full / .65 / .5 / .3 pedal): pavement 143/143/143/112 (unchanged); gravel 105/100/93/69; grass 55/50/44/25; dirt 45/37/28/14; woods 34/27/20/10. Sand and mud are slow on purpose (roughly 15–25 and 10–15 mph).
- **Tyre limit (car only).** Cornering force `v²·tan(steer)/WB` (speedometer units) above 9.5·grip → `S.skid` 0…1 over the next 4 m/s²; also full braking (pedal > .85, less in the wet) or handbrake above 5 m/s. Skid cuts the turn rate by up to 35% (the car pushes wide) and scrubs speed. Dry pavement: none at full lock up to ~35 mph, .36 at 45 mph; grass: .36 at 34 mph. The autopilot stays far below it.
- **Hills.** `a -= 5·sin(S.sp)` when the terrain is on (Medium/High graphics only).
- **Body.** Dive/squat ×1.8 in the car. feel.js sets `S.bx` / `S.bz` (pitch/roll wobble), `S.by` (lift, m) and `S.bj` (jolt 0…1) each frame from smooth noise × surface roughness × speed, plus one jolt when the surface roughness changes (dropping off the pavement) and on impacts; game.js adds them to `body`, the tractor and the car photos (and cockpit.js to the cabin).
- **Solid world** (`SOLID` = REAL and car/tractor). The nose point is `2.4 × max(1, EXAG·.85)` m ahead (behind when reversing).
  - Buildings: nose inside a footprint and the car's centre not inside it → blocked: debris, `FEEL.impact(speed,'wall')`, bounce back at ≤ 3 m/s. Effects only above 1.5 m/s and every .35 s, so leaning on a wall is quiet. `smashing` is held true for .6 s after a hit above 4 m/s so progress.js still counts "Buildings hit" (existing background stat). Already inside a building (restored there, or switched on inside) → the old plow-through, so nobody gets stuck. (Cockpit: footprints from `COCKPIT.blocksAt`, see above.)
  - Trees: `treePts` (game.js, filled by `buildDecor`: trunk x,y pairs in local metres) → `FEEL.hit`: trunk within 1.5 m of the nose and ahead of the car's centre. Dead ahead (< .75 m off line) → stop and bounce; off to one side → once per tree, speed ×.65 and 3° turned away. Trees behind the direction of travel never block.
  - Traffic: each AI car is a box (half length 2.4 × the same scale, half width .9), yours three circles r .9 (front, middle, rear). Only when closing > .5 m/s. Rear-end → it gets a shove, you drop to 80% of its speed; head-on/side → you bounce, it slows and holds 1.2 s (`a.hold`, traffic.js `drive`); it ran into you → it stops 1.5 s. Widths are real-size on purpose: an oncoming car 2 m to the side passes, 1.5 m hits.
  - No penalties or scores for trees/traffic.
- **Sound** (in sound.js's AudioContext, `SOUND.state.ctx`; built lazily; one looped noise buffer → five filters): road hum (lowpass, tone and level rise with speed), grit (bandpass 520 Hz, loose surfaces), wind (bandpass, ∝ speed²), skid squeal (two narrow bands ~720 / 1080 Hz, dry pavement only; hiss when wet; on loose ground a skid adds grit instead), impact = sine drop + noise burst (wall / tree / car variants). Levels `LV` were set in an offline render against the Explorer engine (engine cruise RMS .079): road at 60 mph .035, 120 mph .072, gravel 35 mph .020, full skid .053, impact peaks .3–.7. Silent when stopped, with Sound off, outside car/tractor (an `onModeChange` hook zeroes the gains because frame hooks don't run while gliding).
- **Dust/spray:** one dust puff every .1 s (Low: .2 s) behind the car on loose ground (grass only above 11 m/s), tyre smoke in a skid, spray on a wet road above 10 m/s. Uses game.js `spawn` (the 36-puff pool shared with smashing).
- **Buzz:** `navigator.vibrate` on impacts and surface jolts (Android only; Chrome ignores it until the first tap).
- **Tested:** `node --check`; feel.js in headless Chromium with stubs (surfaces, bumps, tree head-on / glancing / miss / reversing, traffic rear-end / head-on / near miss / T-bone); the real step() block run in node with stubs (0–60 still 5.2 s, skid, grass, wall stop + reverse away + drive out from inside, Realistic off plows through, hill); sounds rendered offline. **Not run on the real map** — the live check is on the owner's phone/PC. Numbers likely to need tuning from how it feels: surface drag, skid threshold, bump size, sound levels.
- **Offered, not built:** skid marks on the road, sliding along walls instead of bouncing straight back, wheelspin on launch, traffic that swerves/honks, rain sound.

## Graphics levels (game.js header, `quality.js`)
- game.js: `QL` 0 Low / 1 Medium / 2 High; `QMODE` 'auto' | 'low' | 'med' | 'high' (localStorage `ydQual`); `QAUTO` {lvl, cap} (localStorage `ydQAuto`); `POWER = QL===0` (every old `POWER` check still means Low); `Q3(low,med,high)`; `CAP30()` = Low, or Medium on a touch device; `COARSE` = `(pointer: coarse)`; `PRS` = adaptive resolution scale; `mapPR()` = min(dpr, 1.25 / 1.5 / 2) × PRS, never below min(dpr, 1); `applyQuality(lvl)` (pixel ratios, terrain on for Medium+High, hillshade only on High, trees rebuilt, then the `onPowerChange` chain — called on every level change, also Medium ↔ High); `setQuality(mode)` (the ⚙️ choice); `setPRScale(k)`.
- First run: old `ydPower` '0' → pinned High; '1' on a PC → pinned Low; otherwise Auto. Auto starts Low on touch devices, High elsewhere.
- Medium values: trees 500 m / 1,200 max / grid 15 m wood, 28 m park; roofs 260 m, 170 houses, 40 big, 6 slices, 110 facade buildings; background ticks 1.5× slower (Low 2.5×). Everything else that asks `POWER` behaves as High on Medium.
- The loop calls `QUAL.frame(now, ran, cost)` on every animation frame (ran = false when the 30 fps cap skipped it).
- `quality.js` Auto (only frames while moving count; 3 s windows of driving): **slow** = average time between drawn frames > 42 ms with the cap, > 30 ms without; **fast** = on time and < 6% of animation frames later than 1.5 refreshes. One slow window → map resolution ×0.85, another → ×0.72 (also on hand-picked levels). Stopped 2 s → full sharpness until you drive off. 30 s of fast windows → one step back (max twice a session). Auto only: 3 more slow windows at ×0.72 → one level down and `QAUTO.cap` lowered so it is never tried again; 2 fast windows at full sharpness → one level up, up to the cap (touch: Medium). Tapping **Auto** in the picker resets the cap. Nothing is judged in the first 10 s, for 3–6 s after a change, or across pauses > 250 ms. Level changes toast "Graphics: Medium" etc.
- ⚙️ row: `#qualPick` (`.seg`, buttons `data-q`), status `#qualStat`; row uses class `carRow` (picker on its own line).
- `?fps=1`: `#fpsMeter` at the left edge — fps, worst frame, game ms (step()), map ms (wraps `map._render`, a MapLibre internal, meter only), level, resolution, sharpness %, last window verdict.
- Stub-tested in headless Chromium with the real header/`applyQuality` cut out of game.js, real settings.js + quality.js, and synthetic frame times: fast phone → Medium after ~20 s of driving; phone that only holds 30 fps on Low → stays Low; fast-at-Low/slow-at-Medium → ×0.85, ×0.72, sharp while stopped, then back to Low with cap 0; slow PC → Medium then Low; 144 Hz PC stays High; hand picks; `?fps=1`. **Not measured on real hardware** — thresholds may need tuning from the owner's `?fps=1` readings.

## Realistic driving (game.js `REAL`, `perfOf`, `grip`, `steerLock`; switch `#realBtn` in quality.js; localStorage `ydReal`, default on)
- `CARS[..].perf = {t60, top (mph), a0}`: Explorer 5.2 s / 143 / 7.8; Javelin (all three) 6.8 s / 120 / 6.0; Navigator 6.1 s / 115 / 7.2. The Navigator's 115 mph and the Javelin's 0–60 are approximate figures; the Javelin's 120 is the owner's number.
- Car mode only (not tractor/boat). Full-pedal thrust = min(a0·grip, pm/v); drag = .18 + cd·v², cd sized so thrust = drag at 1.04 × top; off the gas an engine-braking term (.7 + .015·v) is added. `perfOf(key)` fits pm by bisection so 0–60 matches t60 (Explorer 85.5, Javelin 67.7, Navigator 73.6 W/kg). Throttle = min(1, 1.25·gas), blending to min(1, 2·gas) near top speed, so **half pedal or more still reaches top speed** (the Javelin rule holds).
- Simulated by running the real step() block in node (speeds in mph): Explorer 0–60 5.2 s full pedal / 6.4 s at gas .65 / 8.2 s at .5, 143 mph; gas .3 settles ~116. Javelin 6.8 / 8.3 / 10.7 s, 120 mph (about 49–65 s to get there; was 13–26 s); gas .3 settles ~97. Navigator 6.1 / 7.4 / 9.5 s, 115 mph. Coasting 60 → 5 mph takes ~21 s (was ~12 s).
- **Braking is deliberately unchanged in the dry** (28 m/s² at full pedal): the world moves 3× the speedometer, so that already gives about a real stopping *distance* on the map.
- `wxGrip()` (was `grip()`; 1 unless Realistic driving is on and you're in the car): from `YD.AT.wx` — rain .90–.70 by intensity, snow .55–.40, freezing rain/drizzle (codes 56, 57, 66, 67) .35. `grip()` = that × the surface (see Driving feel). It scales braking, handbrake, launch thrust, and `steerLock(v)` (lock × (1 − (1−grip)·.8·ramp), ramp 0 at 6 m/s → 1 at 20 m/s). Autopilot uses the same `steerLock`, and its curve-speed and brake gain take `grip()`. Toast once when it turns wet ("🌧️ Wet road — brake earlier") or slick ("❄️ Slick road — brake much earlier, turn gently"). Explorer in rain (.76): 0–60 5.7 s, 60–0 full brake 1.2 s (was .9); snow (.45): 8.2 s and 1.9 s. Test with `?wx=63` / `?wx=73` / `?wx=66`.
- The road is wet only while it is raining/snowing (no drying time). MAXV is still the camera zoom reference; the speed cap on road is max(MAXV, the car's top).

## Known limits (added)
- The "Yorkville Driver" artifact copy was **not** republished after aa989ab, d3056a0 or f1fa6df (cockpit); Pages is current.
- Auto's thresholds are untested on real devices. A phone raised to Medium uses more battery than Low (hills, more trees, sharper); pin Low in ⚙️ to avoid it.
- Realistic driving: no wheelspin, weight transfer or per-car braking; all cars share the brake force. Records don't note whether it was on.
- Driving feel: a wall or tree bounces the car straight back (no sliding along it). Only the car's nose (or tail when reversing) is tested against buildings and trees, so the sides can still clip a corner. Trees only exist near the car (the drawn ones), fewer on Low graphics. Hills only act on Medium/High. The surface comes from map land-use data, so a paved lot mapped as "residential" counts as grass, and gravel roads are only known where OpenStreetMap says unpaved. Tyre noise costs a little battery while driving with Sound on. Phone buzz is Android-only.
- Cockpit: interiors are code-built (procedural shapes and textures), so "realistic" means proportions, materials, light and live instruments — not photo-real; the owner's interior photos would be the way closer. The world still moves at 3× the speedometer (SPEEDUP). MapLibre can't look above 5° below the horizon (pitch 85 max) or wider than 60°, and can't roll, so the horizon stays level while the cabin leans. Mirrors show traffic and signs but not the map (no buildings/road surface behind). No hands/arms, no seat/eye-height or FOV beyond 45–60° adjustment, no turn signals, no cockpit for the tractor/boat. Portrait phones see a narrow view (meant for PC/landscape). Uses MapLibre internals (`map.transform.fov`) — check after any MapLibre upgrade.
