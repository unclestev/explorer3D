# Addendum 23 (2026-11-24): swimming, ice skating, hockey rinks

On branch `swim-skate` (test build branch `claude-test-swim`, last 0b6a423). NOT on main yet: waiting for the owner's "push". Tested live in the browser pane (throttled to ~1 fps, so no feel test; no screenshot of the arena was taken that showed the rink up close).

- **Swim / skate on foot (walk.js v=36, ice.js v=1):** water no longer blocks you. `W.medium` is null (land), `'swim'` or `'ice'`, re-checked every 0.1 s (`mediumNow`). Swim: slow front crawl (1 to 2.4 m/s), person lies on the water with a blue pool and ripple rings (`swimFx`). Ice: `ICE.frozen` (AT.temp <= 32 F; `?temp=NN` overrides for tests) turns every water polygon into a pale `ice-fill` layer (just under roads, above the satellite photo; satellite.js skips it in hideCovered and calls `ICE.raise()`), switches off `tx-water` ripples. Skating = velocity vector `W.vx/W.vy` (east/north m/s): push 4.6 m/s^2 up to 9.5 m/s, forward friction low, sideways grip strong, brake = hockey stop. Person gets hockey skates and a stick (`P.skates`, `P.stick`) only on ice.
- **Rinks (rink.js v=1):** arenas come from Overpass (leisure=ice_rink, sport=ice_hockey, or a building named like an ice arena/rink/skating centre) within ~3 km, re-asked after moving 1.5 km; a building counts when it contains one of those points. `RINK.forceHere()` / `RINK.force(rec)` mark any building for testing. Room kind `arena` (high ceiling) gets a regulation 200 x 85 ft rink along the building's long axis (shrunk to fit, min 45 %): painted ice texture, boards + glass with a gate by the nearest door (`blocks()` stops you at the boards elsewhere), two nets, scoreboard (goals/shots), puck. Inside the boards = ice (`onIce`).
- **Puck:** rides on your stick when you skate into it (1.1 m ahead), squirts away if you run into it. New action `shoot` (Space / pad button 0 / the 🏒 `#shootBtn`, shown only in an arena): tap = wrist shot, hold up to 0.9 s = slap shot. Bounces off boards and posts; crossing the goal line between the posts = goal (toast, horn, 2.4 s, puck back to centre dot).
- **Not done / ideas:** outdoor rinks (leisure=ice_rink not in a building) are only ice if they are water; two-player or AI goalie; the player can skate through the nets; no stick visible in first person; arenas with no drawn door can't be entered; frozen water under a car/boat; sounds for skating and splashing.
