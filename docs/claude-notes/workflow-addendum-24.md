## Causes found (Traughber, 41.6702,-88.3302)
- **Fit:** `fit()` took every building ≥260 m2 within 170 m, which included houses 130 m south → plan stretched to 293x148 m over the street, bus loop and lot (IoU .42). Now: pieces ≥1800 m2 (townhouse rows there are ~1000) plus pieces touching them. IoU .65. Orientations within .04 of the best: the one with the gym furthest south-west wins (school records: gym/music suite is the SW wing).
- **Whole subdivision hidden:** the school shares one OpenFreeMap id with ~140 houses. `HIDE.pin` now hides that id only in the map's own layers (game.js `applyHides`, `WALL_LAYERS` excluded); buildings.js walls/roofs skip only the school's own rings via `window.TRJ.skip(ring)` (ring key match, or ring wholly inside the school pieces' bbox — tile cuts differ). **buildings.js has its own local `TRJ` (colours) — use `window.TRJ`.** Walls sig includes `TRJ.ver()`.
- **Gym:** was the bbox of 13 traced boxes → room walls/door 1-2 m off the brick walls. Now the gym is ONE box in the drawn plan (`PLAN` = `PLAN0` minus gym boxes, other wings `cutOut` of it, gym box last = `GYM`). fit() slides the gym box within the plan (±360/240 px, 24 px steps) to where it sits most on the real footprint (96%), so it no longer hangs over the bus-loop drive.
- `gymRec`: doors = main double door (nearest open spot to plan x+75 on the south wall) + 2 m pairs at 28/72% of both long walls **only where the outside is open air** (`cover()` 0.6/2.6 m out); windows (b 4.2, top min(h-1.8,7.4), every 8.4 m) only where nothing stands within 5.5 m outside. `details()` draws exactly these doors (bays) and windows on the brick; the room cuts the same openings. Gym trj features carry `g:1`; `TRJ.hide(true)` filters only those out while you're inside (rest of the school stays visible through windows).
- **Traffic OCC blockers** now include source `trj` as kind `'t'` (so the "building you're in" test drops the gym) and skip the hidden real school rings. Kind `'h'` would have hidden the room's walls from inside (seen live). cockpit.js `buildIndex` (terrain/cockpit collisions) includes `trj` and drops the real school rings.
