# Addendum 28 (2026-11-24): multi-door garages + driveway pad

Branch garage-doors (test commit b6eb1e4, pushed as claude-test-garage2; NOT on main). buildings.js v51.

- Garage count (1-4) is estimated in the house facade code: the garage wall (a wing that juts out, detected from the polygon neighbours, counts its whole face; otherwise 75% of the wall where a driveway reaches it, 60% at the street-side end), the width of the pad in front (PARKING.at run + spread of driveway ends), and the house area (A<130 1, <260 2, <430 3, else 4). Wing: wall width decides; else min(wall, pad or area). Bay unit 3.3 m: nWall=floor((avail+.8)/3.3) so 2 bays from ~6 m of wall, 3 from ~9 m, 4 from ~12.4 m (it was 2.9 m, which gave 353 Emily Ct, a ~12 m wing, two doubles = 4 cars; now 3 doors). A wing wider than its doors scales them up to 1.3x to fill it.
- Door widths: single 2.7 m, double 4.9 m. 2 bays: one double (55%) or two singles. 3: three singles (35%) or single+double either order. 4: double+double (40%), double+2 singles (30%), four singles (30%). If too wide, adjacent singles merge into doubles, then widths scale (min 2.2 m).
- Each door is its own garage object with its own gk (seed*4 + 1,2,3,0), so the opener works on the nearest bay. Lights at both ends of the row; occupy() reserves the whole row for the front door placement.
- Driveway pad (general rule, owner 2026-11-24): the map draws a driveway as a thin asphalt line (#4c4e51 plus a texture, grey kerb), which left grass in front of the doors. Now: a full-width apron (row of doors + 0.35 m each side) out to where the mapped line starts (min 5 m), then a strip that FOLLOWS the mapped driveway line (driveSpot returns the line ordered from the house end, ds.l). Short front yard (street kerb within 12 m, room = sd.d/nd - wall - 6.5): stays full garage width to the street and runs 1.4 m into the street edge so no seam shows. Long yard: full width for 5 m, then narrows gradually to 3.2 m over up to 9 m, and the mapped line carries on. No mapped line: a straight pad (rectangle or trapezoid). Kerb = a slightly larger grey polygon under the asphalt one.
- Colour: extrusion tops render darker than the map's road line, so the pad is #62656a (the road's #4c4e51 looked clearly darker). A faint edge where the pad meets the road remains.
- Surface: asphalt unless the tile property surface is 'unpaved' (gravel #a39b8b). The OpenMapTiles transportation layer only has paved/unpaved, NOT asphalt vs concrete (that is the OSM surface tag; Overpass was unreachable), so concrete driveways cannot be told apart yet.
- Live check at Emily Ct, Yorkville: 353 Emily Ct now 3 doors; houses nearby 1-2 doors. Houses with a garage but no mapped driveway get a straight pad to nowhere (dark patches).
- Untested: interior cut-away (walk.js) with several adjacent bays, night lights, snow on the pad, other regions.
