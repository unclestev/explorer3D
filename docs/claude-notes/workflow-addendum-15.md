# Addendum 15 — parking lots follow the Asphalt setting; continuous sidewalks (2026-11-23)

Owner asked: lots should obey ⚙️ Asphalt roads and look like the roads; sidewalks looked like "spaced out sections of cement" and should be continuous with joint lines. Branch `walks-lots` (test branch `claude-test-walks`). Files: textures.js ?v=12, parking.js ?v=4, roadside.js ?v=11.

## Lots = roads
- New texture `tx-lot` (textures.js): service-road colour #4c4e51 + the road grain baked in (same look as road colour + `tx-asph-*` grain at .22). `window.TXLOT {name(), apply()}`: when `ROADSIDE.state.asphalt` is on, `tx-landuse` (parking class), `pk-stalls`, `pk-padtx` use `tx-lot`, `pk-base`/`pk-pad` fill #4c4e51 and `pk-curb` #9a9a94 (the roads' concrete edge); off = old `tx-parking`, #5f6368, #b9bcc0. `LANDUSE` is now a function (`LANDUSE()`), because its parking entry depends on the setting. `roadside.applyAsphalt()` calls `TXLOT.apply()`; parking.js `layers()` calls it after adding layers.
- Real cause of "white aisles": the style's `road_service_track` layer (service roads: parking aisles, driveways, alleys — AND farm tracks) was skipped by the asphalt code (`SKIP` had `track`). It now gets the asphalt colour via `['match',['get','class'],'track',<original>,<asphalt>]` and the grain layer (filter excludes class track). Its casing gets the concrete edge too.

## Sidewalks
- Two kinds: (1) the **map's own footways** (OSM `highway=footway`, ~1,500 near Yorkville): the style drew them as a white dashed line (`road_path_pedestrian`, dasharray) — that was the "spaced out sections". (2) our synthetic sidewalk along in-town streets (`rs-walk`).
- (1) `footways()` (roadside.js): adds `rs-fw` (solid concrete strip, 1.7 m) and `rs-fwj` (joints: a 0.05-line-width dash every SLAB=1.6 m, from zoom 17) just under `road_path_pedestrian`, and excludes class path subclass footway/cycleway/pedestrian (not bridge/tunnel) from the old dashed layer (trails keep dashes). **Gotcha:** smooth.js moves the style's road layers onto the geojson source `roads-smooth` a moment after load, so layers added earlier to the vector source are rejected ("must specify a source-layer", red error box). `footways()` waits for `map.getSource(pp.source).type==='geojson'`, is idempotent and is retried from the 700 ms tick whenever `rs-fw`/`rs-fwj` are missing.
- (2) `sidewalks()` (roadside.js): pieces of a street are chained end to end per side (mitred corners, no gaps), drawn as one `walk` line; joints (`wjoint`, one MultiLineString per chain) every 1.6 m and tone variation on some slabs (`wslab` t=1 lighter, 2 darker; keyed to distance along the street via `hash`, so they don't shift on rebuild). Pieces within 6 m of a mapped footway are skipped (`mappedWalks()` grid) so streets that already have a mapped sidewalk don't get a second one. Tones in `walkTones()` (asphalt on/off). Build ≈ 20–28 ms per 80 m of driving.
