## Traffic follows the rounded roads, and gives way to you — branch `traffic-curves` (7e85b3e + bfdec96), pushed to test branch `claude-test-traffic-curves`; NOT on main until the owner says "push"
Owner: "make the computer controlled vehicles adhere to the new curved roads so they turn smoothly around curves… also, make the cars yield to the user driver as they approach stop sign intersections or no sign intersections."
- **Bends** (js/enh/traffic.js ?v=32): `corner(s1,s2)` builds the same quadratic bend smooth.js draws (d = min(half of each piece, 2·DEV/sin(θ/2)), DEV 1.8), in the car's lane: P0/P2 = the lane points d before/after the corner, control X = where the two lane lines cross, so it meets the straight lane exactly at both ends. Turns onto another road (node deg ≥3, cos < .9) use d ≥ 10 m. Cached on s1 (`_cn/_cs/_cc`). `pos(a)` now returns [x, y, dirx, diry, m]; heading comes from the curve's tangent; `a.s` advances by v·dt/m so the car moves at its real speed along the bend. `a.prev` = the piece just left (for the second half of a bend). Bend speed cap = SPEEDUP·√(LAT·R), LAT 4 (R = tightest radius); intersection turns also keep the old `turnCap`.
- **Yielding** `yieldTo(a,s,nx,dist,px,py,ph,pv)`: only at nodes deg ≥3 without traffic lights, within 70 m. Holds short (6 m before the node) when you are in the middle (D < 7.5 m; if you sit still there it waits 4 s, then goes), closing on it on a crossing road and would arrive before it or within 4 s after (and within 9 s), or stopped within 16 m facing it (waves you on for 3 s). Ignores you when you're going the same way (behind/ahead), and when oncoming unless the car is turning across your lane. Won't brake if it can't stop (dist−6 > v²/(2·2B)); once it starts holding for a node (`a.yN`) it keeps holding. Stop signs: its 1.3 s stop timer doesn't count while it's yielding. `a.yT` = seconds held at the line.
- Offline harness (node vm, fake graph: 60 m curved road into a T): steady 40°/s yaw through the curve (was a step at each vertex); yields when you'd get there first, goes first when you're well behind; nothing held for a player behind or ahead in the same direction. Note drive()'s argument order is (a,dt,px,py,**pv,ph**).
