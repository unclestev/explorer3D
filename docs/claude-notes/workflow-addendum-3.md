## The "top of a hill" cause (found 2026-11-21)
Owner: "other cars drive off into the horizon (into the sky)". MapLibre 3.6.2 `_calcMatrices` far plane: the law-of-sines ground angle is clamped at ≥0.01 rad (≈0.6°), and the cockpit's bottom padding shrinks its horizon angle further, so from 1.4 m eye height the ground stopped **~27 m ahead**; three.js cars (camera.far 4 km) drew beyond it on sky. The earlier `getHorizon` override could not get past the clamp. Fix (f1fd15a, cockpit.js `wrapTiles`): while in the cockpit, `t._calcMatrices` is wrapped and lowers `t._minEleveationForCurrentTile` (MapLibre's own terrain hook; farZ scales with camera-to-lowest-ground distance) just for that call so the far plane lands past ground `VIEW()` m away; restored right after. Checked against a JS port of the 3.6.2 formula: cut 27 m → 1545 / 2575 / 3605 m for Low / Medium / High. Three.js `camera.far` in the cockpit = VIEW()+60 m. Owner confirmed: "Roads look much better… no more horizon".

## Changes
- **Cockpit hides map labels** (cockpit.js `labels(show)`): every style `symbol` layer except our `sg-*` sign icons is set `visibility:none` on enter (the ones that were visible are remembered in `LBL.hidden`) and restored on leave.
- **Windows on fewer buildings on Low in the cockpit:** buildings.js `maxFac` = Q3(45,150,200) in the cockpit (was 110 on Low).
- **`?hide=` test switch** (cockpit.js, any view, this visit only): comma list of `buildings, roofs, trees, roadside, roads, water, landuse, signs, traffic` hides those layer groups (traffic: `TRAFFIC.state.on=false`, not saved) so `?fps=1` can show what each costs. Toast lists what's hidden.
- **⚙️ 3D terrain** (game.js `TERRMODE`, localStorage `ydTerrain`: `off` | `chase` (default) | `all`; picker `#terrPick` under Graphics): `terrWant()` = not Low && mode ≠ off && not (cockpit && mode ≠ all); `applyTerrain()` turns MapLibre terrain on/off to match (resets `S.sp`/`S.sr` when off). Called from the style load, `applyQuality`, cockpit enter/leave, and the picker. Low never has terrain; a toast says so if picked on Low. In the cockpit the ground is flat unless "Always".
- **Cabin cost trims (02417a5):** `canvasTex(w,h,dyn)` — dynamic canvases (cluster, screen, pano, HUD, rain) have no mipmaps; cluster/pano/HUD redraw only when their value key `c.ik` changes; mirrors 20/15/10 Hz (High/Med/Low); cabin shadow map `autoUpdate=false` in the cockpit, redrawn when heading moves >0.8°, the body rocks, or every 3rd frame (restored to auto on leave); glare background only set when its position changes; building index rebuilt every 150 m or 10 s.
- **Mirrors / hood / beams (ca3fda6, owner: "mirrors on the explorer are just rectangles", "not sure the hood looks realistic", "headlight beams in a more accurate location"):**
  - Explorer/Navigator door mirrors: `mirShape(w,h,o)` rounded-trapezoid glass (short inboard, tall outboard, big lower-outer radius), `uvFit()` for ShapeGeometry UVs, black surround, extruded body-colour shell tapering behind, black foot to the shell's inboard edge, sail triangle in the window corner. Javelin keeps its chrome bullet mirror; tractor keeps rectangular arm mirrors (correct for a tractor).
  - Hood: `hood.crease` (Explorer x .40 h .016, Navigator x .50 h .012) raised centre + painted highlight/shadow lines; outdoor env map has a skyline band (`gradEnv(...,band,low)`) so paint reflects a darker horizon, not only sky.
  - Gaps closed: a dash-to-windshield filler strip (from `P.dash[0]` to the glass base), the cowl now runs under the hood's back edge, and a dark engine-bay floor under the hood (the road showed through all three).
  - Cockpit headlights (atmos.js `makeCkBeams`, `cutCkBeams`; cockpit.js `CK.lamps()` / `LAMPS` per car: lamp offset, front, LED or halogen colour): one 14 × 64 m ground decal from the bumper — low-beam pool, brightest ~15–25 m, reaching ~35 m left / ~60 m kerb side, faded edges; cut short at the first building straight ahead (`COCKPIT.blocksAt` + `contains`). `updateBeams` swaps between chase and cockpit beams on enter/leave/car change. Chase-view beams unchanged.
- **Off the street + trees (4ea8181, owner: "make sure that objects like trees and signs are not on the street… enhance the tree models for realism (round trunks, branches and leaves… some different variety)"):**
  - game.js `ROAD_DRAWN` (half of each road kind's drawn width + curb edge), `roadCells()` grid over `roadGeo.segs`, `roadNear()` (returns `seg` too), `roadClear(lng,lat)` (m past the nearest curb, negative = on the road), `offRoad(lng,lat,gap)` (pushes out of every road band; lands on a corner where roads meet). Used by signs.js (stop / signal / crossing posts via `pt()`, street-name blades in `computeBlades`), POI pins, and to skip woodland/park/roadside trees that would stand on or overhang a street.
  - core.js `mz`/`mzE` now scale metres→pixels to zoom 24 (was 22): the cockpit camera is at zoom 22.5–24, so roads/markings were drawn narrower than life there.
  - Trees: game.js `treeFeats(lng,lat,seed,size,near,out,kind)` — round 8-sided tapering trunk, limbs under the canopy, crown of 6 stacked rounded tiers + 2 side clumps (near) or trunk + 2 tiers (far); maple / oak / spruce / birch; leaf patterns `lf-<hex>` (`addLeafImages()`); `treeSeason()`: autumn Oct 1–Nov 10, bare Nov 11–Mar 31, conifers always green — **owner approved following the seasons**. `TREE_NEAR()` = 55/95/140 m. Layers: `tree-trunk` colour from `c`, `tree-crown` pattern from `p`; roadside.js `rs-crown`.
- **Cockpit real-size signs (972771b, owner: "street lights and stop signs from far away show up real big"):** the `sg-icons` map icons keep one screen size at every distance (fine for chase). In the cockpit they're hidden and signs.js `cockpitSigns` hook draws real-size 3D ones in the car layer from `PTS` (filled in `buildSigns`, with `ang` = direction of the road they stand by): stop 0.76 m octagon (cut-out texture) at 2.1 m, post-mounted signal head (top 4.4 m) on the same 30 s cycle, crossbuck 1.22 m boards at 2.7 m. Face along their road toward the driver's side; occluded via `TRAFFIC.hidden`. Tried live with test signs 25–300 m (Overpass timed out in the pane, so no real OX data). Roadside street lights were already real-size extrusions.
- **No GPU read-backs with terrain (4dcbc21):** game.js `QGEO()` = terrain on or cockpit → `geoBlocks` (COCKPIT.blocksAt index), `geoRoads(lng,lat,r)` (roadGeo; segs now carry `brunnel` at [9]), `geoLand` (landCache.land: landcover/landuse/park polygons within 600 m, ranked) replace queryRenderedFeatures for the wall ahead, road under the car, bridge, land HUD, walk.js checks, signs `roadClassHere`; traffic.js `hiddenGeo` (walks toward the camera checking footprint heights vs the sight line) when terrain on; towers skip occlusion with terrain.
- **Houses at night (4dcbc21):** atmos.js `setWindows` pattern = `['case',['>',H,12],'enh-win','enh-house']` — lit-window grid only on buildings over 12 m, dark lap siding (`enh-house`) on houses; nearest houses' own facade windows still light (buildings.js `lit`). By day houses already had siding/brick colours (textures.js `colourBuildings`) and, nearest ones, windows/door/garage.
