## What this replaces
- **No automatic vehicle switching any more (2026-11-20).** workflow.md says the car becomes the tractor on farmland and the boat on water — both gone.
  - **Tractor** = a car choice: `CARS.tractor` (`tractor:true`, paint 0x367c2b, lamp .55, front 2.1, sound `jd68`), ⚙️ Car → Tractor. Picking it (or any car) switches mode at once in `setCar`; the detection in game.js step() now wants `BOATPICK?'boat':CARSPEC.tractor?'tractor':'car'`. Farmland is just dirt for a car (feel.js); "In the field" shows only for the tractor on farmland. loadPhoto skips cars without `img`.
  - **Boat** = picked while in water: ⚙️ Car → 🚤 Boat (`data-car="boat"` → game.js `pickBoat()`; `BOATPICK` flag). Only works when `S.inWater` (set in the detection from `landCache.water`, bridges excluded). A car/tractor in water wades: drag S.v·1.2, speed clamped −2…2.4 m/s (~5 mph), toast on entering. The boat stops at the shore (probe 3 m ahead not in `landCache.water` → blocked, toast every 6 s); picking any car leaves the boat (wading if still in water). settings.js `showCar` lights Boat while boating and listens to `onModeChange`.
- **SPEEDUP is no longer a constant (b16eeb7):** `let SPEEDUP=3` in game.js; `setSpeedScale(k)` changes it (rescales `S.yr`, traffic road speeds + cars via `TRAFFIC.rescale(r)`, a police chase's speed). It is 1 in the cockpit with ⚙️ Real speed on, 3 everywhere else. Any module must read `SPEEDUP` live (traffic.js braking/accel are now `BB()`/`ACCF()` for this reason) — never cache it at load.
- **roadGeo segments (5ac1ed4):** `roadGeo.segs` entries are now `[x1,y1,x2,y2,half,name,cls,along,lineId]` — `along` = metres from the start of that (tile-clipped) line, counted even for pieces filtered out, so positions measured along a line are stable between rebuilds; `lineId` from `rebuildRoadGeo.n`. `nearRoadGeo()` sets `roadGeo.hit` {name, cls}.
- **Cockpit view covers the tractor** (not the boat): `COCKPIT.active()` = want && (car || tractor). Tractor cab spec `SPECS.tractor` (style 'tractor', eye (0, 2.46, .9), pitch 76, single wiper) built by `tractorCab()` instead of `shell()`/dash: hood with vents and stripes, stack + pre-cleaner, front tyres that steer/roll (`parts.tyres`), rear fenders + tyres, four posts, roof/headliner/overhead console, glass all round, column display (drawSport), corner-post map screen, armrest console, door mirrors. tractorG is hidden while inside and restored on leave.
- **Explorer (sport) cockpit restyled (owner sent stock photos of a 2020+ Explorer ST interior and asked for it to look "exactly like this").** Claude declined an exact copy (Ford's design; same line as the Javelin model and traffic cars; the photos looked like dealer stock photos, not the owner's own, and the repo is public) and built an original interior "in that spirit": two-round-dial digital cluster (`drawSport`: rev counter left, speed right, digital speed / street or turn / limit / trip / PRNDL in the middle), centre screen standing proud of the dash, wide vents with metal trim (two beside the screen, one each end), climate row with two metal knobs, flat-bottom wheel with a satin U-shaped lower spoke and dark switch pads. No badges or logos. Don't move it closer to the real Ford layout.
- **On-screen controls hide with a wheel:** wheel.js `showRow()` toggles `body.wheel` whenever a wheel is found; CSS `body.wheel #ctl{display:none}` (steering slider + pedals). They return when it's unplugged. If the pedals aren't set up, use keys or ⚙️ Wheel → Set up pedals.
- **Settings panel order:** Car (… Navigator · Tractor · 🚤 Boat) · View · Real speed in cockpit · Head-up display · Cockpit view angle (those three only with View = Cockpit) · Graphics · Realistic driving · Asphalt roads · Traffic · … (rest as in addendum 1).
- **Module list:** … `quality.js`, `feel.js`, `cockpit.js`, `roadside.js`, then `boot.js`.

- Trade-offs given for the remaining options:
  - **5A. Upgrade MapLibre** (sky, fog/haze, better terrain): one or two sessions + device testing; must recheck everything using MapLibre internals (tower beacons, cockpit `coveringTiles`/`getHorizon`/`transform.fov`, the `?fps=1` map timer); the map still looks like a map.
  - **5B. Own three.js 3D world built from the live map data** (textured roads with lanes, curbs, terrain, fog, shadows; better physics on real ground): the real path to ATS-like visuals, but effectively a second engine (months of evenings), every map-based feature must move over or coexist, surfaces are generic (no photo data), likely PC-only or a reduced phone mode, browser performance < native.

## Roadside detail + asphalt roads (`js/enh/roadside.js`, `window.ROADSIDE`; 5ac1ed4)
- One GeoJSON source `roadside` (maxzoom 19) with layer `rs-walk` (line, sidewalks, inserted under the first road casing so roads paint over crossings) and `rs-3d` (fill-extrusion, vertical gradient off, before `tree-trunk`). Features carry `k` (kind), `b`/`h` (base/height m), `c` (colour). Real size (not EXAG), so buildings occlude them correctly; flat-earth base (terrain drapes them).
- Built from `roadGeo.segs` within R = Q3(150,220,280) m of the car; rebuilt (700 ms tick) after moving 80 m (Low 120) or when `roadGeo.t` changed and moved > 25 m; not in glider mode. Skips motorway/track/path/service. Positions are every `step` m along each line measured with `along` (stable). Clearance: not within another road's half-width + margin (same line within 60 m ignored), not in/against a building (`querySourceFeatures(bSrc,'building')` footprints on a 25 m grid).
- "In town" = any building centroid within 45 m of the segment midpoint. Out of town: wooden utility poles (10.2 m, cross-arm 2.4 m at 9.3 m, insulators) one side of the road (side = hash of the road name/class) every 48 m at half+3.2 m, wires (3.5 cm slabs at 9.55 m) between consecutive poles on the same line/side < 60 m apart; Medium/High also a tree (trunk + octagon crown) with 28% chance every 26 m, 5–12 m back. In town: street lights every 38 m (trunk/primary/secondary, alternating sides) or 52 m (others, one side) at half+1.1 m, 8.2 m pole, 2.2 m arm, lamp head (`k:'lamp'` → `#ffd58a` when `YD.AT.k > .5`); sidewalks both sides (1.8 m wide, centre at half+2.2 m); fire hydrants every 85 m (random side) at half+0.9 m; mailboxes (post + box, 4 colours) at the curb in front of each building ≤ 600 m² whose centroid is 3–35 m beyond the road edge, on minor/tertiary streets, one per building.
- Stub-tested in node (country road into a town street with 20 houses + a side road): 32 poles/insulators, 28 wires, 8 trees, 6 lamps, 4 sidewalk lines, 2 hydrants, 20 mailboxes; plotted top-down and checked. Not run on the real map.
- **Asphalt roads** (⚙️ Asphalt roads `#asphaltBtn`, localStorage `ydAsphalt`, **on by default**): `road_*`/`bridge_*` transportation line layers (not rail/path/track/ferry/etc.) get asphalt colours by class (#36383b motorway … #4c4e51 service), casings concrete #8a8b86 / #9a9a94; `tx-asph-*` grain overlay opacity .45 → .22; sidewalks a bit darker. Original colours are remembered (`RS.orig`) and restored when off. Applied once on ready (don't re-apply on `styledata` — setPaintProperty fires it → loop).

## Cockpit additions (2026-11-20, after the first cockpit commit)
- **View distance (83bdf01):** owner: "in cockpit view… processing too much data… pull significantly fewer map tiles while enriching the closer detail". cockpit.js `wrapTiles()` wraps `map.transform.coveringTiles` once (MapLibre internal; recheck after any MapLibre upgrade): while `COCKPIT.on`, tiles whose nearest edge is farther than `VIEW()` = Q3(1500, 2500, 3500) m from the eye (`CK.eyeLL`) are dropped (`CK.tiles` = [before, after]); if none survive, the first is kept. No horizon haze: a screen-space band striped nearby buildings at eye height (tried and removed); proper haze needs MapLibre fog (5A). Near detail in the cockpit: game.js `buildDecor` radius Q3(250,340,430) with grids ×0.7 (denser trees); buildings.js roofs radius Q3(160,210,250), facade buildings Q3(110,150,200). enter/leave call `refreshNear()` (`decorAt=null`, `ROOFS.state.pending=true`). Mirrors ≤ 30 Hz on all levels.
- **Draw to the view distance (b16eeb7):** `wrapTiles()` also overrides `map.transform.getHorizon` while `COCKPIT.on`: instead of MapLibre's tan(90°−pitch)·D·0.85 (which put the far plane ~100 m ahead from the seat), it returns tan(a − atan(eyeH/VIEW())) · D (a = 90°−pitch, eyeH = `CK.eyeH`), so the ground and buildings are drawn to the tile limit. Checked in a stub only (depth precision at that range is the risk to watch).
- **Framing (ff3a7c6):** default vertical view angle 45° (was 60; ⚙️ Cockpit view angle 30–60, localStorage `ydCkFov2`, the old `ydCkFov` is ignored), default pitch 81 (Explorer, Navigator), 82 (Javelin), 76 (tractor) instead of 85, so the horizon sits about a third down with more road; drag up to raise it (pitch clamp 50–85). HUD .6 × .25 m placed 2.6° above each car's hood line (`atan((eye.y−hood.yf)/(eye.z−hood.zf))`).
- **Real speed in the cockpit (b16eeb7):** ⚙️ Real speed in cockpit (`#rsBtn`, row `#rsRow`, localStorage `ydRealSpd`, **default on**): enter → `setSpeedScale(1)`, leave → `setSpeedScale(3)`; toggling inside applies at once. Nav ETA, progress miles, autopilot, police, traffic and feel all read SPEEDUP live.
- **Racing-wheel steering (b16eeb7, game.js step()):** with wheel input (`WH.ok`, keys/touch idle, autopilot off, not boat) the lock is a fixed `rad(450/15.5)` ≈ 29° (900° wheel, ~15.5:1) at all speeds instead of `steerLock(v)`, and the rack follows at dt·24 (was 9). With ⚙️ Realistic driving, yaw rate `S.yr` follows the kinematic rate with a .13 s lag (`S.hdg += S.yr·dt`). Keys, touch and autopilot keep `steerLock`. No force feedback (browser limit); recommend G HUB centering spring.
- **Realism (2d6f8fe):** head motion — a damped spring on the eye: sideways −lat/9.8·4.5 cm (lat = v²·tan(steer)/WB), fore-aft from dv/dt (braking → forward), slight drop with it; applied to both the map/three camera and the cabin camera. Windshield reflections (shell cars only, `parts.refl`): grey dash veil low on the glass, opacity (.05+.12·sun on dash)·(1−k); additive blurred blobs where the cluster/screen/pano/pod would reflect, opacity .32·k. Sun glare: DOM `#ckGlare` (z 6, screen blend) — when the sun is within ~57° of the view and a raycast from the eye toward it (every 200 ms) hits nothing opaque in the cabin; strength by alignment, low sun, cloud, daylight. Cabin sound: sound.js routes the engines (and feel.js tyre/wind) through `A.bus` lowpass → `A.boom` peaking 110 Hz → destination; `SOUND.cabin(inside, tractor)` sets 1.7 kHz / +3.5 dB (tractor 2.6 kHz / +2 dB) in the cockpit, open outside; cockpit re-applies it if sound starts later (`CK.snd`).
- **Head-up display (057a910; not in the Navigator since ff820ca — owner: its dash screen already shows it):** a 768×320 canvas on a plane (now .6 × .25 m, see Framing) ~2.3 m ahead of the eye, additive, depthTest off, renderOrder 20, fixed to the car (stays ahead of the seat when looking round), dims .45 at night. Shows speed + MPH, rev bar (red past `red`), gear, speed-limit ring (red when > limit+4), next-turn arrow + distance. On by default; ⚙️ Head-up display (`#hudBtn`, row `#hudRow` shown only with View = Cockpit), H key in the cockpit; localStorage `ydHud`.
- **Graphics level in the cockpit:** Low = no cabin sun shadows (sun dimmed inside), car-layer pixel ratio 1.25, mirrors 15 Hz, view 1.5 km; Medium = shadows 1024, 1.5×, 30 Hz, 2.5 km; High = shadows 2048, 2×, 30 Hz, 3.5 km. Interior geometry/textures/screens are the same at every level (owner declined adding more to High).

## Autopilot and traffic (`autopilot.js` `traffic()`; 8078b03)
Owner: "sometimes gets stuck behind a car and it just stops there… navigate around other cars". Cause: it drove the route's centre line ignoring traffic, bumped cars ahead/oncoming, and feel.js makes a bumped car hold → deadlock.
- Keeps right: pursuit target offset `A.off` (smoothed, .8/s; 3/s while passing) to `A.lane` = ROAD_HALF[class]/2 (0.9–3.6 m) from `roadGeo.hit` (refreshed every .5 s via `nearRoadGeo()`); passing lane = −A.lane. Pursuit look-ahead shortens to max(4.5, 3.5+.3·vw) while changing lanes.
- Follows: lead = same-direction AI car within |lateral| < 2.1 m ahead → target ≤ cap(its speed·.95, gap−14 m).
- Passes when blocked > 2.5 s behind a car going < 1 m/s or < 45% of the limit, the left lane is clear of oncoming cars for 170 m and of same-way cars from −15 to 40 m, and no route stop sign/light lies between us and 35 m past it (unless blocked > 12 s). While swinging out the passed car still counts as a lead but we creep at ≥1.2 m/s while > 7 m from it. Abort (tuck back) if an oncoming car comes within 90 m while it's still ahead. Back in once it's 9 m behind and the right lane is clear. Toast "🚗 Autopilot passing". Anything heading straight at us within 70 m → stop short.
- Simulated in node (straight 2-lane road): stopped car passed with ~4 m clearance; 9 mph car passed; 25-in-a-30 car followed, not passed; oncoming car at 420 m → waits, then passes. Not tried on real streets (bends, intersections). Note the yaw lag (b16eeb7) also applies to autopilot steering when Realistic driving is on.

## Known limits (added)
- The boat no longer goes on land at all; a lake whose water polygon isn't loaded yet near the shore can stop the boat early or late. No cockpit for the boat.
- Autopilot passing is untested on real bends/intersections; it doesn't pass on curves deliberately (no check), and ignores lane markings (it uses the road class width).
- Cockpit view distance: buildings beyond 1.5–3.5 km don't exist in the cockpit (a far skyline/water tower beyond that is missing). No distance haze (needs MapLibre fog). Windshield reflections and glare are screen/texture fakes, not ray-traced. No tractor windshield reflections.
- With real speed on, trips in the cockpit take 3× longer than in the chase view; switching views mid-drive changes the world speed at once.
- Roadside objects are guessed positions (map data has no poles/mailboxes/curbs), straight-line wires (no sag), flat-earth bases; town/country is a 45 m building test, so a lone farmhouse makes a short "town" stretch. Sidewalks follow the centre-line offset, so on odd-width roads they can sit on the road edge or in the grass.
- The "Yorkville Driver" artifact copy still not republished; Pages is current.
